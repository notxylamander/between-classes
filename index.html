    Leave-by reminders

    Get a phone notification when it's time to walk to your next class, through Google Calendar. Reminders use your current classes, walking pace, and starting place, so download them again after changing those.

    How to add them to Google Calendar

    Do this on a computer: Google Calendar's phone app can't import files.

    1. Open calendar.google.com.
    2. Click the gear (Settings), then Add calendar → Create new calendar. Name it On Time and create it.
    3. Under Settings for my calendars, choose On Time, then Event notifications. Set one notification for 5 minutes. Google uses this, not the time in the file.
    4. Go to Import & export, choose the downloaded file, set Add to calendar to On Time, and click Import.
    5. On your phone, check that the Google Calendar app is allowed to send notifications.

    To change your reminders later, remove the On Time calendar (Settings → On Time → Remove calendar → Delete), then repeat these steps with a new download.
